## 1. LABORATORY ATTENDANCE AND EXPERIMENT COMPLETION

### 1.1 Strict 75% Laboratory Attendance
- Practical laboratory sessions demand hands-on technical competency; hence the **75% minimum attendance rule** is enforced without waiver.
- Students must complete **100% of all scheduled laboratory experiments** in the syllabus.
- If a student misses a laboratory session due to approved medical leave, they must attend a scheduled **Make-up Laboratory Session** during the designated buffer week at the end of the semester.
- Failure to complete at least **85% of practical experiments** by the final lab session will result in disallowance from the End-Semester Practical Examination.

### 1.2 Laboratory Evaluation Breakdown (50 Marks)
Laboratory courses are evaluated continuously throughout the semester:
- **Weekly Experiment Execution & Data Collection**: 20 Marks (assessed every session based on technique and precision).
- **Continuous Laboratory Record / Notebook**: 10 Marks (reviewed weekly by the lab instructor).
- **Mid-Semester Practical Skill Test**: 10 Marks.
- **End-Semester Practical Viva-Voce & Execution**: 10 Marks.
- **Passing Mark in Laboratory**: A student must achieve at least **50% overall** (25/50 marks) to receive a passing grade.

### 1.3 Laboratory Safety and Dress Code
- Students must wear cotton laboratory coats (white for Chemistry/Biology, navy blue for Mechanical/Civil/Electrical workshops).
- Closed-toe leather or safety shoes are mandatory; sandals and open footwear are strictly prohibited.
- Willful damage to instruments or lab equipment results in an immediate equipment replacement surcharge equal to **150% of the replacement cost** plus disciplinary review.
