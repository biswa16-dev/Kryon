# UNIVERSITY ACADEMIC REGULATIONS (ACADEMIC YEAR 2024–2025)
**Document ID:** REG-2024-V2.1  
**Effective Date:** July 1, 2024  
**Applies To:** All Undergraduate (B.Tech, B.S.) and Postgraduate (M.Tech, M.S.) Programs

---

## SECTION 1: PROGRAM STRUCTURE AND CREDIT REQUIREMENTS

### 1.1 Credit Definition
- One lecture hour per week per semester equals **1.0 Academic Credit**.
- One tutorial hour per week per semester equals **1.0 Academic Credit**.
- Two laboratory or practical hours per week per semester equal **1.0 Academic Credit**.
- A student must earn a minimum of **160 credits** to be eligible for the award of a 4-year Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) degree.
- For B.Tech with Honours, a student must successfully complete an additional **20 credits** through approved advanced/research courses or MOOCs with prior Dean (Academics) approval.

### 1.2 Semester Load and Course Registration
- The normal academic load per semester is **20 to 24 credits**.
- The minimum credit registration in any regular semester is **16 credits**.
- A student on Academic Probation may register for a maximum of **18 credits** per semester.
- Course add/drop requests must be submitted within **10 instructional working days** from the commencement of the semester.
- Course withdrawal without academic penalty (recorded as grade 'W' on the transcript) is permissible up to the **end of the 8th week** of the semester.

---

## SECTION 2: ATTENDANCE POLICIES AND CONDONATION

### 2.1 Minimum Attendance Mandate
- A student is expected to attend 100% of all lectures, tutorials, and laboratory sessions.
- In no case shall a student with less than **75% aggregate attendance** in a course be permitted to appear for the End-Semester Examination in that course.
- In courses having an integrated laboratory component, a student must maintain at least **75% attendance separately** in both the theory and the laboratory components.

### 2.2 Condonation of Attendance Shortage
- Condonation of attendance shortage between **65% and 74.9%** may be granted by the Academic Council under exceptional circumstances:
  - Serious medical illness requiring hospitalization, supported by a government medical officer certificate and discharge summary submitted within 5 working days of resumption of classes.
  - Representation of the University in authorized national or international sports, cultural, or technical competitions.
- A non-refundable condonation fee of **₹1,500 ($25)** per course is levied upon approval.
- Attendance below **65% cannot be condoned under any circumstances**. Students with attendance below 65% receive the grade **'FA' (Failed due to Attendance)** and must re-register for the course in a subsequent semester.

## SECTION 3: ASSESSMENT AND GRADING SYSTEM

### 3.1 Continuous Internal Assessment (CIA) and End-Semester Exam (ESE)
- Theory courses are evaluated on a **100-mark scale** divided as:
  - Continuous Internal Assessment (CIA): **40 Marks** (two Mid-Term Tests of 15 marks each, plus 10 marks for assignments/quizzes).
  - End-Semester Examination (ESE): **60 Marks** (comprehensive 3-hour written examination).
- To pass a course, a student must obtain:
  - A minimum of **40% marks** in the End-Semester Examination (i.e., at least 24/60 marks).
  - An overall aggregate of at least **45% marks** in CIA + ESE combined.

### 3.2 10-Point Letter Grading Scale
The university adopts a 10-point absolute and relative grading scale:

| Letter Grade | Grade Points | Qualitative Description | Marks Range (Reference) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **O** | 10.0 | Outstanding | ≥ 90% |
| **A+** | 9.0 | Excellent | 80% – 89% |
| **A** | 8.0 | Very Good | 70% – 79% |
| **B+** | 7.0 | Good | 60% – 69% |
| **B** | 6.0 | Above Average | 50% – 59% |
| **C** | 5.0 | Pass | 45% – 49% |
| **F** | 0.0 | Fail (Re-appear) | < 45% |
| **FA** | 0.0 | Failed due to Attendance | Mandatory repeat |
| **I** | 0.0 | Incomplete (Medical emergency during ESE) | Converted within 30 days |
| **W** | 0.0 | Withdrawn | Not counted in GPA |

### 3.3 SGPA and CGPA Calculation
- **Semester Grade Point Average (SGPA)**:
  `SGPA = Σ (Course Credits × Grade Points) / Σ (Course Credits)` for all courses registered in that semester.
- **Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA)**:
  `CGPA = Σ (Total Earned Credits × Grade Points) / Σ (Total Registered Credits)` across all completed semesters.

---

## SECTION 4: ACADEMIC STANDING, PROBATION, AND HONORS

### 4.1 Academic Probation Criteria
- A student whose CGPA falls below **5.00** at the end of any academic year will be placed on **Academic Probation**.
- A student on probation is assigned a Faculty Mentor and cannot hold elective office in student bodies.
- If a student remains on probation for **two consecutive semesters**, the student's registration is suspended for one academic year.

### 4.2 Degree Classification
- **First Class with Distinction / Honours**: CGPA of **8.50 and above**, passed all courses in the first attempt within the standard 4-year duration.
- **First Class**: CGPA of **6.75 to 8.49**.
- **Second Class**: CGPA of **5.00 to 6.74**.

---

## SECTION 5: RE-EVALUATION AND SCRUTINY

### 5.1 Re-evaluation Process
- A candidate may apply for verification of marks or full re-evaluation of answer scripts within **15 calendar days** from the date of declaration of results.
- The prescribed fee for script scrutiny is **₹500** per course, and for re-evaluation is **₹1,200** per course.
- If the mark awarded in re-evaluation differs from the original mark by more than **15%**, the script is referred to a third independent examiner, and the average of the two closest marks becomes final.
