### Course Descriptions

**CSE111: Orientation to Computing I**
- **Credits:** 2
- **Prerequisite:** None.
- **Core Topics:** Introduction to computer systems, computer hardware and software, operating systems, number systems, binary arithmetic, computer architecture fundamentals, computational thinking and problem-solving approaches.
- **Course Objective:** Introduce students to fundamental computing concepts and develop basic computational literacy.

**MEC136: Mechanical Engineering with AutoCAD**
- **Credits:** 4
- **Prerequisite:** None.
- **Core Topics:** Engineering mechanics fundamentals, engineering drawing, orthographic projections, isometric projections, geometric constructions, dimensioning, sectional views, 2D drafting and 3D modelling using AutoCAD.
- **Course Objective:** Develop fundamental mechanical engineering knowledge and computer-aided engineering drawing skills.

**CSE326: Web Development**
- **Credits:** 2
- **Prerequisite:** None.
- **Core Topics:** Introduction to the World Wide Web, HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript fundamentals, DOM manipulation, forms, hyperlinks, responsive web design, website structure and basic client-side programming.
- **Course Objective:** Enable students to design and develop basic interactive websites.

**MTH165: Mathematics for Engineering**
- **Credits:** 4
- **Prerequisite:** Basic school-level mathematics.
- **Core Topics:** Differential calculus, integral calculus, limits and continuity, differentiation, applications of derivatives, matrices, determinants, complex numbers and elementary differential equations.
- **Course Objective:** Establish mathematical foundations required for engineering and computing disciplines.

**PHY110: Physics for Engineering**
- **Credits:** 3
- **Prerequisite:** Basic physics.
- **Core Topics:** Wave optics, interference, diffraction, polarization, electromagnetic waves, quantum physics fundamentals, semiconductors and engineering applications of physics.
- **Course Objective:** Develop an understanding of physical principles relevant to modern engineering technologies.

**INT108: Python Programming**
- **Credits:** 4
- **Prerequisite:** None.
- **Core Topics:** Python syntax, variables, data types, operators, conditional statements, loops, functions, strings, lists, tuples, dictionaries, sets, file handling, exception handling, modules and object-oriented programming fundamentals.
- **Course Objective:** Develop programming and computational problem-solving skills using Python.

### Course Descriptions

**CHE110: Environmental Studies (EVS)**
- **Credits:** 2
- **Prerequisite:** None.
- **Core Topics:** Environmental science, ecosystems, biodiversity, natural resources, environmental pollution, climate change, sustainable development, waste management and environmental protection.
- **Course Objective:** Develop environmental awareness and understanding of sustainable engineering practices.

**CSE101: C Programming**
- **Credits:** 4
- **Prerequisite:** Basic computing knowledge.
- **Core Topics:** C programming fundamentals, data types, operators, conditional statements, loops, arrays, strings, functions, pointers, structures, unions, dynamic memory allocation and file handling.
- **Course Objective:** Develop structured programming skills and establish a foundation in computer programming.

**CSE121: Orientation to Computing II**
- **Credits:** 2
- **Prerequisite:** CSE111 or equivalent introductory computing knowledge.
- **Core Topics:** Advanced computing fundamentals, computer organization, memory hierarchy, operating system concepts, algorithms, data representation, networking fundamentals and emerging computing technologies.
- **Course Objective:** Expand fundamental computing knowledge and prepare students for advanced computer science courses.

**CSE320: Software Development**
- **Credits:** 3
- **Prerequisite:** Basic programming knowledge.
- **Core Topics:** Software development life cycle (SDLC), requirement analysis, software design, software testing, debugging, version control using Git, software maintenance, agile methodologies, project planning and software documentation.
- **Course Objective:** Introduce software engineering methodologies and practical software development processes.

**ECE249: Electronics and Communication Engineering**
- **Credits:** 3
- **Prerequisite:** Basic mathematics and physics.
- **Core Topics:** Semiconductor devices, diodes, transistors, rectifiers, amplifiers, digital electronics, logic gates, Boolean algebra, communication systems and electronic circuit fundamentals.
- **Course Objective:** Develop fundamental knowledge of electronic components, circuits and communication technologies.

**ECE279: Electronics and Communication Engineering Practical**
- **Credits:** 1
- **Prerequisite:** ECE249 or concurrent enrolment where permitted.
- **Core Topics:** Electronic component identification, diode characteristics, transistor characteristics, rectifier circuits, logic gates, digital circuit implementation, measurement instruments and laboratory safety.
- **Course Objective:** Provide practical experience in electronic circuits and laboratory experimentation.

**INT306: Database Management Systems (DBMS)**
- **Credits:** 4
- **Prerequisite:** Basic programming and computing knowledge.
- **Core Topics:** Database fundamentals, relational database models, ER diagrams, relational algebra, SQL queries, joins, constraints, normalization (1NF, 2NF, 3NF, BCNF), keys, transactions, ACID properties, indexing and database security.
- **Course Objective:** Develop skills in designing, implementing and managing relational databases.

**MTH166: Mathematical Calculus**
- **Credits:** 4
- **Prerequisite:** MTH165 or equivalent mathematical foundation.
- **Core Topics:** Advanced differential and integral calculus, partial differentiation, multiple integrals, vector calculus, sequences and series, convergence, applications of integration and mathematical modelling.
- **Course Objective:** Strengthen mathematical foundations required for engineering, computing and advanced analytical applications.

**PEL125: Upper Intermediate Communication Skills**
- **Credits:** 3
- **Prerequisite:** Basic English communication proficiency.
- **Core Topics:** Advanced grammar, vocabulary development, reading comprehension, professional writing, presentation skills, group discussions, listening comprehension, interpersonal communication and workplace communication.
- **Course Objective:** Improve professional English communication and interpersonal skills.

### Course Descriptions

**CSE202: C++ Programming**
- **Credits:** 4
- **Prerequisite:** CSE101 or equivalent programming knowledge.
- **Core Topics:** C++ fundamentals, object-oriented programming, classes and objects, constructors, destructors, inheritance, polymorphism, encapsulation, abstraction, function overloading, operator overloading, pointers, dynamic memory allocation, templates, exception handling, file handling and STL.
- **Course Objective:** Develop object-oriented programming skills and prepare students for advanced software development and algorithm implementation.

**CSE205: Data Structures and Algorithms (DSA)**
- **Credits:** 4
- **Prerequisite:** CSE101 or equivalent programming proficiency.
- **Core Topics:** Arrays, linked lists, stacks, queues, recursion, searching, sorting, trees, binary search trees, heaps, hashing, graphs, graph traversal algorithms (BFS and DFS), shortest path algorithms, algorithm analysis, time complexity and space complexity.
- **Course Objective:** Develop efficient problem-solving and algorithm-design skills using fundamental data structures.

**CSE276: Artificial Intelligence (AI) – AI & ML Specialisation**
- **Credits:** 3
- **Prerequisite:** INT108 (Python Programming) and basic mathematical knowledge.
- **Core Topics:** Introduction to Artificial Intelligence, intelligent agents, problem-solving techniques, state-space search, uninformed search, informed search, heuristic algorithms, knowledge representation, propositional logic, predicate logic, reasoning systems, expert systems, machine learning fundamentals and AI applications.
- **Course Objective:** Introduce intelligent systems and computational techniques used to solve real-world problems through Artificial Intelligence.

**CSE306: Computer Networks**
- **Credits:** 3
- **Prerequisite:** CSE111 or equivalent computing fundamentals.
- **Core Topics:** Network fundamentals, network topologies, OSI reference model, TCP/IP architecture, physical layer, data link layer, network layer, transport layer, application layer, IP addressing, subnetting, routing, switching, TCP, UDP, DNS, HTTP, network security and wireless communication.
- **Course Objective:** Develop an understanding of computer network architecture, communication protocols and data transmission.

**INT335: Design Thinking**
- **Credits:** 2
- **Prerequisite:** None.
- **Core Topics:** Introduction to design thinking, empathy mapping, problem identification, user research, ideation, brainstorming, prototyping, testing, innovation methodologies, user-centred design and real-world problem-solving.
- **Course Objective:** Develop creativity, innovation and structured approaches to solving real-world problems.

**MTH401: Discrete Mathematics**
- **Credits:** 4
- **Prerequisite:** MTH165 and basic mathematical reasoning.
- **Core Topics:** Mathematical logic, propositional logic, predicate logic, sets, relations, functions, mathematical induction, combinatorics, permutations, combinations, recurrence relations, graph theory, trees, Boolean algebra and discrete structures.
- **Course Objective:** Establish mathematical foundations for algorithms, computer science theory, cryptography and computational reasoning.

**PEL134: Upper Intermediate Communication Skills II**
- **Credits:** 3
- **Prerequisite:** PEL125 or equivalent communication proficiency.
- **Core Topics:** Advanced professional communication, technical writing, business correspondence, report writing, public speaking, professional presentations, group discussions, interview preparation, negotiation skills and workplace communication.
- **Course Objective:** Strengthen advanced communication, presentation and professional interaction skills.

# 2. CSE CORE BRANCH – SEMESTER 3 SPECIALISATION

The first two semesters have a common curriculum for CSE Core and CSE (AI & ML) students.

In Semester 3, the specialisation course differs:

| Course Code | CSE (AI & ML) | CSE Core |
|---|---|---|
| CSE276 / equivalent | Artificial Intelligence | Cloud Computing |
| Credits | 3 | 3 |

### Cloud Computing – CSE Core Specialisation

- **Credits:** 3
- **Prerequisite:** Basic programming and computer systems knowledge.
- **Core Topics:** Introduction to cloud computing, cloud service models (IaaS, PaaS, SaaS), cloud deployment models, virtualization, virtual machines, cloud storage, distributed computing fundamentals, cloud architecture, resource management, scalability, cloud security and introduction to cloud platforms such as AWS, Microsoft Azure and Google Cloud.
- **Course Objective:** Develop an understanding of cloud infrastructure, service delivery models, virtualization and distributed computing technologies.

*Note: The official course code for Cloud Computing should be confirmed from the CSE Core curriculum. CSE276 is used here only for the AI branch course supplied in the curriculum.*

# 4. COURSE REGISTRATION AND PREREQUISITE WAIVER POLICY

## 4.1 Prerequisite Enforcement
- Students are expected to satisfy the prescribed prerequisites before registering for advanced courses.
- The academic registration system may automatically verify prerequisite completion.
- Students who have not passed a prerequisite course may be restricted from registering for dependent courses.
- Exceptional prerequisite waivers may be considered with approval from the course instructor and Head of Department or designated academic authority.

## 4.2 Course Add/Drop Policy
- Students may add or drop courses within the university-prescribed registration period.
- Course withdrawal shall follow the academic calendar and applicable university regulations.
- Withdrawal after the prescribed deadline may result in academic consequences.

## 4.3 Course Repeat Policy
- Students failing a course may be required to re-register or appear in a subsequent examination.
- Attendance and internal assessment requirements for repeated courses shall follow university regulations.
- Backlog clearance must be completed within the maximum duration permitted for the degree.

# 6. ACADEMIC LEARNING OUTCOMES

Upon successful completion of the first three semesters, students are expected to demonstrate the following competencies:
1. **Programming Proficiency:** Ability to develop programs using Python, C, and C++.
2. **Web Development:** Ability to create basic responsive and interactive websites.
3. **Data Management:** Understanding of relational databases, SQL, and database design.
4. **Software Engineering:** Knowledge of software development processes, testing, and project management.
5. **Computer Science Fundamentals:** Understanding of computing systems, algorithms, and data structures.
6. **Engineering Foundations:** Mathematical, physical, and electronic engineering knowledge.
7. **Specialisation Knowledge:** Introductory knowledge of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning or Cloud Computing.
8. **Professional Skills:** Effective communication, teamwork, innovation, and problem-solving.
9. **Analytical Thinking:** Ability to apply mathematical and computational methods to engineering problems.
