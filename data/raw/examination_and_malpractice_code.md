# CODE OF EXAMINATION DISCIPLINE AND MALPRACTICE PENALTIES
**Document ID:** EXAM-CODE-2024  
**Authority:** Office of the Controller of Examinations  
**Revision:** 4.0 (2024)

---

## CHAPTER 1: EXAMINATION HALL ADMISSION AND CONDUCT

### 1.1 Hall Ticket Eligibility
- No student shall be admitted to the examination hall without a valid **Physical/Digital Hall Ticket** and the **University Identity Card**.
- Hall tickets are issued only to candidates who satisfy the 75% attendance threshold and have cleared all university dues.
- Candidates must occupy their designated seats at least **15 minutes** before the scheduled commencement of the examination.
- Late entry is strictly prohibited after **30 minutes** from the commencement of the examination.
- No candidate is permitted to leave the examination hall during the **first 60 minutes** or the **final 15 minutes** of the session.

### 1.2 Permitted and Prohibited Articles
- **Permitted Items**: Blue/black ballpoint pens, pencils, erasers, non-programmable scientific calculators (models fx-991MS / fx-82MS or equivalent specified in course rubric), and clear water bottles without labels.
- **Prohibited Items**: Mobile phones, smartwatches, Bluetooth earbuds, fitness trackers, programmable calculators, loose sheets of paper, scribbled formulas on pencil cases or clothing.
- Possession of prohibited electronic devices in the exam hall (even in switched-off state) constitutes a **Category II Malpractice**.

## CHAPTER 2: MALPRACTICE CLASSIFICATION AND PENALTIES

The Examination Malpractice Prevention Committee (EMPC) classifies offenses into four severity categories:

### Category I: Minor Infractions
- **Offenses**: Possessing unauthorized blank sheets, conversing with neighboring candidates, copying rough notes from desk surfaces, refusing to show hall ticket upon invigilator demand.
- **Penalty**: Cancellation of the examination of that specific course. The candidate is awarded an 'F' grade in the course and may appear in the subsequent supplementary examination.

### Category II: Severe Copying and Electronic Contraband
- **Offenses**: Possession of handwritten crib notes, textbook snippets, mobile phones, or smart wearable devices during the examination; sharing answer scripts with another candidate.
- **Penalty**: Cancellation of the performance in that course PLUS cancellation of all examinations registered in that semester. Debarred from registering for any examination for **one full subsequent semester**.

### Category III: Organized Cheating and Tampering
- **Offenses**: Smuggling answer booklets into or out of the examination center, replacing answer sheets, using generative AI tools via concealed transmitters, tampering with marks or invigilator signatures.
- **Penalty**: Cancellation of all examinations of the current semester, debarment from the university for **two consecutive academic years**, and forfeiture of institutional scholarship.

### Category IV: Impersonation
- **Offenses**: Arranging an imposter to write an examination or appearing as an imposter on behalf of another candidate.
- **Penalty**: Permanent expulsion of the student from the university rolls, lodging of a First Information Report (FIR) under the Indian Penal Code / relevant state impersonation acts, and lifetime disqualification.

## CHAPTER 3: SUPPLEMENTARY AND MAKE-UP EXAMINATIONS

### 3.1 Eligibility for Supplementary Exams
- Students with grade 'F' in any course may appear for the Supplementary Examination conducted during the summer recess.
- A maximum of **5 backlog courses** may be registered in a single supplementary examination term.
- The registration fee is **₹800 ($12)** per backlog course.

### 3.2 Make-up Examination for Medical Absentees
- If a candidate with >75% attendance misses an End-Semester Examination due to documented hospitalization or acute bereavement in the immediate family, they receive an **'I' (Incomplete) grade**.
- A special Make-up Examination is conducted within **30 days** of semester result publication. Upon completion, the 'I' grade is converted to the earned letter grade without grade penalty.
